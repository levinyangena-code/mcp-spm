The implementation roadmap begins with package/CLI foundation and proceeds through discovery, assessment, risk and persistence, reporting, monitoring/API, and hardening. Dashboard work is later-term. Dynamic assessment is opt-in and sandboxed. M0 adds documentation and structural planning only; it does not implement these modules.
